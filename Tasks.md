Signature Assignment – Economics 201 – Macroeconomics
• Select a country of interest to you
• Answer the following questions
1. Using a reliable data source, such as the World Bank World Development
Indicators,
a. Look at the country’s GDP per capita, as well as indicators of quality of life,
such as life expectancy or literacy.
b. Compare the country to the average for its region.
c. Does the country’s GDP per capita seem to be a good indicator of its
standard of living? (one way to think of this is to ask “If the GDP per capita is
high or low compared to the regional average, are the quality of life statistics
also high or low compared to the regional average?”)
2. Look at the country’s real GDP over the last 20 years (if you can’t get data for
the most recent 20 years, get the most recent 20 years available).
a. Has the country experienced any recessions during that time? Illustrate this
with a graph. (For many, this will be the COVID recession or the 2008-10
recession)
b. Select one of the recessions that the country experienced.
i. Discuss what might have led to the recession in the country.
ii. Specifically talk about any components of aggregate expenditure that
might have been affected, and what affected them.
3. Take a look at the components of GDP using the IMF’s National Accounts
data.
a.What percentage of GDP do each of the components of aggregate
expenditure (C, I, G, X, M) represent?
i.Create a table that gives these percentages over the last 15 years.
ii.What trends do these percentages exhibit over time?
4. What would you expect to happen to the government spending (G) percentage of
GDP after a recession? Do you see this reflected in your chosen country’s data?
• Your answers to the questions should be written in the form of a report or briefing.
o The report should have headings separating the sections.
o The questions should be answered and explained with clearly written prose.
o Your sources should be cited in the text, and the full bibliographic
information should be included in a Bibliography at the end of the report
o Every sentence that cites a fact that you don’t know personally should
have an in-text citation of the source that supplied the fact.
(For example: “The government countered the recession with income
support programs (Smith, 2021).”)
o Each source listed in the Bibliography should include the author, title,
publication, and date. A link is not sufficient, as links go dead quite
frequently.
